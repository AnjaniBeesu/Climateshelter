04 — Field guide

Know the signals.

Small actions can reduce exposure. If symptoms are severe or worsening, seek urgent medical help.

SIGNAL / A

Heat exhaustion

Heavy sweating, weakness, headache, dizziness, nausea, or muscle cramps can be warning signs. Move to a cooler place, loosen clothing, and sip water if alert and able to drink. Get medical help if symptoms worsen or don't improve.

COOL + REST
SIGNAL / B

Heat stroke

Confusion, fainting, seizures, or very hot skin can indicate a life-threatening emergency. Call emergency services immediately. Cool the person rapidly with cold water, wet cloths, or fanning while help is on the way.

ACT NOW
SIGNAL / C

Reduce exposure

Shift strenuous work away from peak heat where possible. Seek shade, wear lightweight clothing, keep water close, check on neighbours, and plan routes around shaded or air-conditioned places.
