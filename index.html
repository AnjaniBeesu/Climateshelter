04 - Field guide

Know the signals.

Small actions can reduce exposure. If symptoms are severe or worsening, seek urgent medical help.

Signal / A

Heat exhaustion

Heavy sweating, weakness, dizziness, headache, nausea, or muscle cramps. Move to a cooler place, loosen clothing, cool the body, and sip water if fully alert.

Signal / B

Make room to cool

Take breaks in shade or an air-conditioned space. Drink water regularly and reduce strenuous activity during the hottest part of the day.

Signal / C

Heat stroke is an emergency

Confusion, fainting, seizures, or very high body temperature need immediate emergency help. Call local emergency services and actively cool the person.
